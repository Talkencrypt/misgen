Welcome To Misgen Auto Parts

Misgen Auto Parts has been serving the community since 1972. We are pleased to be your single source for u-pull services, aftermarket parts, auto recycling, and we have a great selection of used vehicles. If you are in the area, you may stop by our location in Ellendale, Minnesota, to our U-Pull yard to find the part you have been searching for. With our Parts Search software, we can help you find that hard to find the part and have it shipped directly to you. Our recycling services help you clean out the garage and get rid of unwanted vehicles. We provide used and like-new vehicles, so stop by our location today and find what you are looking for. Feel free to call us at 800-358-4902 for more information.

U-Pull Parts

Please bring your tools and pull the parts you want from our U-pull section. The U-pull has the cars and trucks you drive and fix, mainly ten years old and older.

Parts Search

Our warehouse and late-model section have thousands of inventoried parts that you can search online. We remove the parts for you to pick up here or to ship to you.

Recycling

We want your dented, rusted, and worn-out cars and trucks. We pay based on quality and parts value. Towing service is also available.

For Sale

We have a variety of cars and trucks For Sale at our location in Ellendale, Minnesota. Please stop by to see our inventory or browse our great selection here.
